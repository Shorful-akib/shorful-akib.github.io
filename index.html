Biography

I am a researcher from Bangladesh with an MSc in Statistics from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST). My work focuses on Bayesian inference, empirical Bayes methods, and hierarchical modeling for sparse and structured data, with particular interest in borrowing strength across related populations to improve estimation when information is limited. My recent research applies and evaluates Bayesian and empirical Bayes approaches to adult mortality estimation using Demographic and Health Survey (DHS) data, including network-adjusted estimators and comparisons of exchangeable and age-structured priors. This work reflects my broader interests in hierarchical modeling, uncertainty quantification, and statistical learning. I am seeking a fully funded PhD to deepen my training in Bayesian statistics, hierarchical modeling, and statistical machine learning, while developing methodology with applications across health, demography, and related fields.
