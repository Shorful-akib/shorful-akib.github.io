Projects & Skills

Statistical & Computational Work

Statistical & Bayesian Methods

Bayesian & Empirical Bayes Methods

Hierarchical Bayesian modeling using R-INLA, including IID and RW2 priors and penalized-complexity priors; empirical Bayes methods using SURE-based heteroscedastic shrinkage; posterior inference.

Small-Area Estimation

Fay–Herriot models, area-level modeling, residual smoothing, model-based estimation for sparse and heterogeneous data.

Statistical Inference & Computation

Survival analysis, Monte Carlo simulation, design-based bootstrap variance estimation, regression, hypothesis testing, time-series analysis.
