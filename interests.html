Research Interests

Areas of Focus

Bayesian statistics and statistical machine learning form the methodological core of my work; the application domain is deliberately open.

Bayesian Statistics

Probabilistic modeling that formally incorporates prior knowledge and quantifies uncertainty — the foundation of my methodological approach across projects.

Empirical Bayes

Estimating prior distributions directly from data to stabilize noisy, sparse estimates — the core technique in my DHS mortality estimation work.

Hierarchical & Small-Area Models

Borrowing strength across related units — age groups, regions, or survey domains — to produce stable estimates where individual cells are too sparse to trust alone; the central methodological question in my comparison of exchangeable and age-structured priors for small-area mortality estimation.

Bayesian Computation

Currently working with R-INLA for fast, deterministic approximate inference in hierarchical models; actively extending this toward full MCMC-based estimation in Stan to work with a broader class of models beyond the latent Gaussian framework INLA is built for.

Survival Analysis

Modeling time-to-event data, including age-specific mortality, under incomplete or indirect observation.

Statistical Methodology for Sparse & Complex Data

How prior structure, shrinkage, and borrowing strength interact with survey design, sampling variance, and data sparsity — the throughline connecting my published and current work.

Demography & Health Statistics

Quantitative methods for estimating mortality and population health outcomes from household survey data, including DHS-based adult mortality estimation and analyses of health service utilization and disparities.

Statistical Machine Learning

Supervised and unsupervised methods — ensembles, clustering, dimensionality reduction — as a complement to classical statistical inference; an area I'm keen to build on with the same rigor I bring to Bayesian methodology, across whichever application domain that leads to.

Open to PhD research opportunities in Bayesian statistics, biostatistics, and demographic/health data science.
